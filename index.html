🥯 Round with holes in it 🍩

Every bagel and donut shop in New York City, on one map.

Data: OpenStreetMap via Overpass API. Includes places tagged shop=bagel, shop=doughnut, cuisine=bagel/donut, or with "bagel"/"donut"/"doughnut"/"Dunkin'"/"Krispy Kreme" in the name or brand, clipped to New York City's administrative boundary — so New Jersey and Long Island shops that fall inside a bounding box are excluded. OSM coverage is uneven — some shops are missing, a few entries may be outdated. Chain flag catches Dunkin' and Krispy Kreme by name or brand. Refreshed weekly. Source on GitHub.
